# 0.5.x

## 0.5.3 — 2026-10-09

### Fixed

- Camera switching now restarts the existing LiveKit track instead of opening a second camera before releasing the first. This supports phones with exclusive camera hardware while retaining the existing encrypted publication.
- Front/back switching uses reported facing mode rather than requiring two enumerated devices. Desktop cycling and explicit device selection remain available.
- Serialize repeated flip taps, cancel switches on stop/leave, and attempt to restore the original device if switching fails. Failure messages no longer incorrectly claim that there is no other camera or that recovery always succeeded.
- Automated restart, restoration, and cancellation checks pass; physical front/back switching remains unverified on the user's phone.

## 0.5.2 — 2026-10-09

### Fixed

- Attach camera and screen tracks that subscribed before the video mixer was created. Late joiners no longer depend on the sharer restarting a stream to see it.
- Give the voice dock its own reserved layout space instead of a sticky overlay over participant tiles. Only the media stage scrolls when needed.
- Wrap narrow-screen controls and replace the long settings label with an accessible icon on phones. Shrink participant tiles during screen sharing so ordinary two-person sessions fit.

### Verified

- Layout-only Chromium fixture checks at 320px/393px and 1200×700 show no horizontal overflow or dock overlap; the two-participant desktop fixture requires no stage scrolling. These checks do not prove real phone media delivery.

## 0.5.1 — 2026-10-09

### Changed

- Camera device, resolution, and FPS dropdowns use themed custom menus instead of native browser/OS pickers. Includes 44px touch targets, selected-state highlighting, keyboard navigation, Escape, outside-dismissal, and dynamic camera options.

## 0.5.0 — 2026-10-09

### Added

- Independent camera and screen resolution (360p–1440p) and frame-rate (15–60 fps) preferences, remembered per account/device. These are capture requests, not guarantees, and apply on next start.
- Camera device selection and an explicit shared-audio toggle. The browser share picker must also permit audio.
- Tap/click or keyboard-expand video into a focused viewer with fit/fill and supported fullscreen controls. Remote shares have a volume slider; zero mutes shared audio without changing microphone playback or bypassing deafen.
- Sharing status explicitly indicates whether shared audio was captured.

### Fixed

- Backend integration also needs same-origin camera permission in Permissions-Policy: the previous `camera=()` blocked browser capture independently of UI or user consent.

- Screen audio tracks are now published and released alongside their video instead of discarded. The server must grant `SCREEN_SHARE_AUDIO` for new room tokens.
- Camera and screen previews remain visible simultaneously, survive tile rebuilds, and are not removed by remote-only reconciliation.
- Stop/leave cancels pending capture results and rolls back late or failed publications. Browser stop-sharing releases screen audio too.
- Duplicate subscriptions detach their old elements, and focused viewers close when their source is removed.

### Validation limits

- Automated lifecycle, placement, preferences, and viewer tests do not establish real two-client encrypted media delivery. Live validation remains pending while the backend stays down.
- Native Android screen capture, direct-call video, desktop-native capture selection, and live quality changes remain outside this release. No unencrypted fallback was added.
